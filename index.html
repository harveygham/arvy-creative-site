About Me

Hi, I'm Harvey — the man behind the camera.

“Capturing the energy of a moment as it happens.”

I'm a videographer and drone operator who loves capturing the energy of a moment as it happens, whether that's in the crowd or on stage at a festival, capturing every emotion of a wedding day, or watching from a few hundred feet up in the air.

I work closely with every client to understand the story they want to tell, then shoot and edit quality films that feel natural, cinematic and true to the day.

When I'm not filming, you'll usually find me with my camera or drone close by, because some things are better captured in the moment! You might also find me running to my favourite playlist, most likely pushing my pace when it gets to a good part of a song 🎵
