About Me

HG

Hi, I'm Harvey — the man behind the camera.

I'm a videographer and drone operator who loves capturing the energy of a moment as it happens whether that's in the crowd at a festival, the high emotion of a wedding day, or a sweeping shot from a few hundred feet up.

I work closely with every client to understand the story they want to tell, then shoot and edit quality films that feel natural, cinematic and true to the day.

When I'm not filming, you'll usually find me carrying my camera or drone somewhere nearby, or you might catch me running 🏃
